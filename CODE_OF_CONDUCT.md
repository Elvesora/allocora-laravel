# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, protect private information, and focus review discussions on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other abusive behavior are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's published contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
